Summit Pro subscription

  • Summit Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, at the price shown in the app before you subscribe. Prices are shown in your local currency and include any applicable tax.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase or, if you start with the free week, when the free week ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically at the same price for the same period unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within the 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings (Settings, your name, Subscriptions). Cancelling stops the next renewal; Summit Pro stays on until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • If you are offered a free week and do not cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the subscription begins and you are charged. Any unused part of a free week ends when you buy a subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its own policies, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Health

Summit is for motivation and fun. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. The health figures it quotes come from published research and are general, not about you. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you have a health condition, and climb stairs safely.

Accuracy

Summit's climbing comes from the Flights Climbed Apple Health records, and its peaks, routes and heights are drawn from public maps and terrain data. They are a guide, not a survey, and may differ from the real thing.
